Deep down, in the deep blue ocean resides a bustling environment of sea creatures and plants, and if you’re lucky you might find something more magical. Above us swim graceful hammerhead sharks and schools of colorful fish that glimmer as they move. An octopus crawls with stealth through the vibrant corals, in search of prey hiding in the shadows. Tiny crabs scuttle chaotically when the shadow of a big creature comes over them, but it is just a peaceful whale shark and nothing to worry about. The ocean is blue as can be, except for the occasional shimmer caused by the flicking tail of a mermaid.
